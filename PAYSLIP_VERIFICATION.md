# Payslip Verification - RShotsBilling

## Design Standards
The payslip follows the official Kenyan professional standard:
- **Header**: Branded with Logo, Name, Address, KRA PIN, and Payroll Period.
- **Body**: Split into clear sections:
    - **Employee Details**: Name, ID, PIN, Department, Bank Info.
    - **Earnings**: Basic, Overtime, Bonus, Allowances, Gross Pay.
    - **Deductions**: PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, Loans, Advances.
    - **Employer Contributions**: NSSF, SHIF, AHL (clearly visible but not part of net pay).
- **Summary**: Highlighted Net Payable amount in KES.
- **Footer**: System timestamp, "Generated by RShotsBilling", and digital stamp.

## Data Interconnection
Every value on the payslip is directly sourced from the `PayrollEntity` processed by the engine, ensuring 100% consistency with the Payroll Register and P9 Forms.

## Verification Checklist
- [x] Logo scale and alignment.
- [x] Correct rounding of PAYE.
- [x] Visibility of all statutory deductions.
- [x] Proper display of zero values (blanks for zero earnings/deductions).
- [x] Branded footer with generator metadata.
