# P9 Form Verification - RShotsBilling

## Form Accuracy
The generated P9 form matches the standard KRA layout:
- **Column A**: Basic Salary.
- **Column B**: Non-Cash Benefits.
- **Column C**: Value of Quarters.
- **Column D**: Total Gross Pay (A+B+C).
- **Column E1**: Affordable Housing Levy.
- **Column E2**: SHIF/SHA.
- **Column E3**: NSSF (Standard Contribution).
- **Column G**: Interest on Owner Occupied Property (Mortgage).
- **Column K**: Taxable Pay.
- **Column L**: Tax Charged.
- **Column M**: Personal Relief.
- **Column N**: Insurance Relief.
- **Column O**: PAYE Tax.

## History Integration
The form is generated by iterating through the payroll history for the specified year. Missing months are automatically padded with zeros/dashes to maintain the 12-month structure.
